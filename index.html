Your data is yours. We never see it.

Security was the first decision, not a feature added later. There is no account to create. Bank sync, AI, backups and the web view each run on an account you set up yourself. Read the privacy policy

  • Stays on your phone

    No account and no server of ours. Everything is stored on the phone and works in airplane mode.

  • Locked to you

    Turn on the lock and open it with Face ID, Touch ID or your fingerprint. It locks again after a minute away.

  • Encrypted backups

    Daily backups to your own Google Drive or a folder you pick, sealed on the phone with AES-256-GCM before they leave. The key comes from a passphrase only you know, so nobody else can read them.

  • Keys in secure storage

    Your bank key, broker keys and sessions, AI key, ChatGPT sign-in and backup passphrases live in the phone’s Keychain or Keystore, or are encrypted with a key kept there. They stay on that phone and never go into a backup.

  • Only what is needed leaves

    Categorising sends a merchant name and your category names. The assistant sends what it needs to answer, amounts included, and tells you first. Price lookups send a ticker. Drive access covers only the app’s own files, and folder backup can open only the folder you picked.

  • Nothing collected

    No analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no tracking. We never see a single record.

  • You see what is sentBefore anything leaves, the app lists it: the merchant and whether money went in or out, plus your category names. Never an amount, date, account, note or label.
  • You decideSuggestions wait for you to apply or dismiss them. Turn on Automatic and new records are filed while the app is open. Unsure answers are never applied.
  • Your key stays on the phoneThe API key or ChatGPT sign-in lives in the phone’s secure storage and never goes into a backup.

And the small things that make it yours.

  • Categories and subcategoriesSupermarket and Bakery roll up into Groceries.
  • LabelsTags that cut across categories, like Holidays or Family.
  • Your coloursTwelve colours for accounts, categories and labels, and an icon for each account and category.
  • Find it, fix it in bulkSearch by text, amount, date range, account, category or label, then change the category, account or labels of many records at once.
  • Hide amountsOne switch turns every figure into dots, for checking in public.
  • Light and darkFollows your phone, or pick one yourself.
  • English and PortugueseThe app speaks European Portuguese too. It follows your phone, or pick a language in Settings.
  • Accounts in your orderEvery list and picker follows the order you set.
  • Any currencyEach account keeps its own currency, with nothing converted behind your back.
  • New phone, same moneyRestore a backup from a file, a folder or Drive on the first screen.
  • Back up to a folderiCloud Drive or any Files folder on iPhone. Any folder on Android, including one Syncthing, Nextcloud or a cloud drive app keeps in sync.
  • A nudge to back upAfter your first account, the app asks to turn on automatic backups. A calm card shows when they are off, failing or more than a week behind.
  • Paid back, not incomeWhen a friend pays you back, the expense shrinks to your share.
  • Your own serviceSelf-host the optional service for a read-only view in the browser and an MCP server your AI tools can read.
  • Bring your historyMove over from Realbyte, or bring in XTB and broker statements, with a file prepared on a computer.
