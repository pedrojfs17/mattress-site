Investments

Holdings, the trades behind them and what they are worth. Investments are part of Mattress Pro.

Add a holding

  1. Open the Investments tab and tap Add holding.
  2. Pick an account that holds investments. Any account can, with Holds investments on.
  3. Type the symbol, the name, the currency and the kind, or use Look up a symbol.
  4. Leave Record the first buy now on to enter the first trade: date, quantity, price, fees and the cash the broker moved.

Open a holding to see your position, cost and gains. Tap Add trade for each later buy or sell.

Prices

Once a holding has a symbol, the phone fetches its closing prices from Yahoo Finance. Open Settings, then Quotes, to see the last refresh or refresh now. A holding with no price keeps its cost and is never counted as zero.

Brokers that sync

Trade Republic, Trading 212 and Robinhood can bring trades in on their own. Open Settings, then Bank connections, then Add connection. Choose where the shares go with Shares go to: an account that holds investments, in the same currency.
