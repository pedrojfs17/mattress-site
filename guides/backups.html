Mattress

Backups

Your records live only on your phone. A backup keeps a copy if the phone is lost or reset.

Your passphrase

Every backup is encrypted with a passphrase you choose. It cannot be recovered, and without it no backup can be opened. Write it down somewhere safe.

Three ways to back up

  • Google Drive backup. Open Settings, then Google Drive backup, then Connect Google Drive. The app sees only its own files, in a folder called "Mattress backups".
  • Backup to a folder. Open Settings, then Backup to a folder, then Choose a folder. Any folder works, including iCloud Drive or one a sync app keeps.
  • A backup file. Open Settings, then Backup and restore, then Export backup, and save the file where you like.

Drive and folder backups run on their own, at most once a day and only when something changed. They keep the 7 newest, then one a week for 4 weeks and one a month for 12 months. Back up now runs one at once.

Restore a backup

  1. Open Settings, then Backup and restore.
  2. Pick Choose a backup file, Choose from Google Drive or Choose from a folder, and enter the passphrase.
  3. Check what the backup holds, then confirm.

If the phone already has data, restoring replaces it. The app first asks you to save a backup of what is there now. Nothing is merged.

Bank keys and logins, AI keys and payment capture permissions are not in a backup. After a restore, set them up again. Your bank links and card mappings are kept.
