Records and the review inbox

Add what you spend in seconds, and clear what needs you in one place.

Add a record

  1. Tap the plus button at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Choose Expense, Income or Transfer.
  3. Type the amount, then pick the account and the category. Add labels or a note if you want.
  4. Save it. The balance moves at once.

A transfer moves money between two of your accounts. It has no category and does not count as spending.

Find records

The Records tab lists every record for the period at the top. Search by merchant or note, or use Filters to pick a category. Tap a record to edit or delete it.

The review inbox

When something needs you, Overview says how many things, with Start review. The inbox takes them one at a time:

  • new records from your bank that need a category,
  • captured payments that are not finished,
  • money back, like a refund, to link to the expense it pays back,
  • changes from your bank to a record you already have.

One tap answers each one. Skip what you are not sure about; it waits for next time.

Less to review next time

On a record, Make a rule from this record files the same merchant for you from then on. See categories, labels and rules.
