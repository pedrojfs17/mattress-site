Accounts

Every record goes in an account. Start with the one you pay from.

Add an account

  1. Open the Accounts tab and tap Add account.
  2. Give it a name and pick its Type: Current account, Savings, Credit card, Investment, Loan or Other.
  3. Set the currency, as three letters such as EUR. It cannot be changed later.
  4. Set the Starting balance: what the account held before your first record in Mattress.

Good to know

  • Totals are kept per currency and never converted.
  • Leave out of reports keeps the account out of your spending and income totals. Its balance stays visible. See Stats for the exceptions.
  • Holds investments lets the account hold investments. It starts on for investment accounts, and works on any type.
  • An account with records cannot be deleted. Archive it instead: it keeps its records and balance, and leaves reports and pickers.
  • Reorder accounts on the Accounts tab sets the order used in lists and pickers.
  • Money borrowed or lent goes under Debts, on the Accounts tab.
