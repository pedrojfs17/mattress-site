Stats

See where the money went. Stats are drawn from your records.

The sections

Open the Stats tab. It has six sections:

  • Spending: your pace against the previous period, where the money went, the largest expenses and when you spend.
  • Trends: spending by month and by category, against your usual.
  • Cash flow: where money comes from and goes, fixed against discretionary, and your recurring payments.
  • Balance: account balances over time, net worth and your savings rate.
  • Labels and payees: spending by label and your top payees.
  • Statement: income and expenses by category, next to the previous period.

Pick what you see

Change the period at the top, or open Filters to pick accounts. Tap a figure, then See records, to see the records behind it.

What is left out

Stats count accounts in euros only, and nothing is converted between currencies. Transfers, archived accounts, accounts left out of reports and categories that do not count in reports are left out too. Two exceptions: picking one account in Filters shows it even if it is archived or left out of reports, and net worth counts every account you still hold.
