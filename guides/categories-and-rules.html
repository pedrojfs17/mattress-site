Categories, labels and rules

Sort records once, and let rules do it from then on.

Categories

Open Settings, then Categories. Add default categories adds a starter set and keeps yours. Add category makes your own.

  • Pick a Parent to make a subcategory. Subcategories add up into their parent in reports.
  • Turn off Count in reports for money that is not really spending. Its records and balances stay.
  • Records can stay uncategorised.

Labels

Labels are tags across categories, like a trip or a project. Open Settings, then Labels, then Add label. A record has one category and up to five labels.

Rules

A rule spots a merchant and files it for you, like your bakery as Groceries. Open Settings, then Rules, then Add rule.

  1. Under When a record, choose what to look at (Merchant, Note, or Merchant or note), how it matches (contains, is exactly, starts with) and the text.
  2. Under Then set, choose a category, labels, or a cleaner merchant name.
  3. Optionally, limit it to one account or to expenses or income only.

Rules are checked from the top, and the first match wins. They never overwrite a field you typed yourself. Apply to records you already have shows a preview before anything changes.

AI categorisation

With Mattress Pro, AI can suggest categories for what your rules miss. Open Settings, then AI categorisation, and use your ChatGPT plan or your own API key. Only the merchant name, whether the money came in or went out, and your category names are sent. Suggestions wait for your review, unless you turn on Automatic, which files them for you while the app is open.
